# Sarthak Sattigeri

I am a B.Tech Computer Science & Biosciences student at Manipal University Jaipur, working on ML evaluation, vision-language models, and robotic manipulation.

My current research focus is on testing where frontier and open-weight models fail when they are pushed from language-level reasoning into grounded physical tasks. I am especially interested in VLM affordance prediction, robotic grasping, egocentric video understanding, and multilingual LLM behavior.

## Research Interests

- Vision-language model evaluation for robotics and physical affordances
- Robotic manipulation, grasping, and part grounding
- Multilingual LLM evaluation, especially Hindi and Indian-language settings
- Egocentric video, IMU signals, and activity boundary detection
- Reliable benchmarks with simple baselines, reproducible code, and failure-mode analysis

## Selected Work

- [VLM Force Prediction](https://github.com/S1rlvk/VLM-Force-Prediction): Zero-shot benchmark testing whether a VLM can infer robotic grasping force from images; includes physics-based ground truth, fragile-object safety analysis, and result scripts.
- [Phase-Conditioned Trajectory](https://github.com/S1rlvk/Phase-Conditioned-Trajectory): Research code for phase-conditioned Kalman filtering of hand trajectories and next-phase prediction experiments.
- [WEAR IMU Segmentation](https://github.com/S1rlvk/wear-imu-segmentation): IMU vs. I3D vs. fusion experiments for subtask boundary detection using 18-subject LOSO cross-validation.
- [IODarkWatch](https://github.com/S1rlvk/IODarkWatch): Open-source maritime domain awareness project for detecting suspicious vessel behavior and AIS gaps in the Indian Ocean region.
